# OAS Solutions Ltd — Corporate Website

Corporate website for OAS Solutions Ltd, a Nigerian technology and product company building digital products across mobility, faith, education and property.

## Portfolio
- OkRide — flagship mobility product — https://okride.com.ng
- Gospira — faith and digital ministry platform — https://gospira.org
- Study Companion — education product in development
- School Manager — education-management product in development
- RentEasy — property technology product in development

## Legal architecture
The corporate site includes a Legal & Trust centre with corporate Privacy, General Terms, Cookie Policy and Data Protection pages, plus OkRide-specific Passenger Terms, Rider Terms and Safety Policy.

## Technology
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, designed for lightweight Cloudflare deployment.

## Important
Legal documents are product/legal drafts and should be reviewed by qualified Nigerian legal and data-protection professionals before being relied upon as legal advice. Operational claims, retention periods, security controls, vendor lists and product-specific rules should be verified against production systems before publication.
