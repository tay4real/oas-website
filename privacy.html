1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how OAS Solutions Ltd (“OAS”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information in connection with oassolutions.com.ng and OAS corporate communications and services. Product-specific services may have additional privacy notices that apply to their particular data processing.

OAS is a technology company based in Akure, Ondo State, Nigeria, and develops digital products including OkRide and Gospira, with additional products in development.

2. Information we may collect

Information you provide

  • Name, email address, phone number and organisation details when you contact us or request a partnership.
  • Information included in messages, applications, feedback or other communications.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic device, browser, IP address, access time and technical information needed to operate and secure the website.
  • Usage information generated when you interact with the website, subject to our Cookie Policy.

Product information

When you use an OAS product, additional information may be processed according to that product's own privacy notice and terms. For example, OkRide may process trip and location information necessary to provide mobility and safety functions.

3. Why we process personal data

Depending on the context, OAS may process personal data to provide requested services, respond to enquiries, maintain security, prevent fraud and misuse, improve products, communicate service information, comply with legal obligations, establish or defend legal claims, and perform other purposes described at the point of collection.

Lawful basis: Where required by applicable data-protection law, OAS determines an appropriate lawful basis for each processing activity, which may include performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interests, consent, or another lawful basis recognised by applicable law.

4. When we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers acting on our instructions, professional advisers, public authorities where legally required, or other parties where necessary to provide a requested service or protect users and the platform.

Examples may include cloud hosting, communications, payment processing, mapping, analytics or security providers. Product-specific notices provide more detail where appropriate.

5. International processing and transfers

Some OAS technology providers may process information using infrastructure located outside Nigeria. Where personal data is transferred across borders, OAS will take appropriate steps and safeguards required by applicable Nigerian data-protection law.

6. Security

OAS uses reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks involved. These may include access controls, authentication controls, secure transmission, logging, backups, vendor controls and security monitoring.

No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe your information or an OAS account has been compromised, contact us promptly.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions, you may have rights including:

  • to be informed about processing;
  • to request access to personal data;
  • to request correction of inaccurate data;
  • to request deletion where applicable;
  • to object to or request restriction of certain processing;
  • to request portability where applicable;
  • to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • to seek redress or lodge a complaint with the appropriate authority.

See our Data Protection & Your Rights page for the request process.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations. Product-specific retention periods may differ and will be described in the relevant product notice.

9. Children and young users

Different OAS products may have different age and eligibility requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the corporate website where such collection is not intended. Products designed for students or younger users will provide additional safeguards and age-appropriate notices where applicable.

10. Cookies

The corporate website may use essential cookies or similar technologies needed for functionality, security and preferences. See our Cookie Policy for details.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as our products, technology or legal obligations evolve. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
