OkRide
MOTORCYCLE RIDE-HAILING · ONDO STATE

OkRide is a two-sided marketplace connecting passengers with identity-verified motorcycle riders — built around live GPS trip tracking, transparent upfront pricing, an SOS safety flow, and automated Paystack split settlement (85% rider / 15% platform). Piloting in Akure, with expansion across Ondo State in view.

STATUS — the backend, database, admin approval workflows, and the core rider/passenger trip flow are built and have been demonstrated end-to-end on physical devices. Rider onboarding and identity verification is the next operational phase, ahead of a live pilot.
