OkRide Rider Terms

Product-specific terms for riders participating on the OkRide mobility platform.

Last updated: 19 August 2026 · RC No. RC7765644

On this page

EligibilityObligationsAvailabilityCommercial termsSafetyContact

1. Eligibility and verification

Riders must meet the eligibility, identity, licensing, vehicle and safety requirements communicated by OkRide. Verification information must be truthful, current and supported by valid documentation.

2. Rider obligations

  • Maintain required licences, registration and documentation.
  • Operate a safe and roadworthy motorcycle.
  • Follow applicable traffic and safety requirements.
  • Treat passengers professionally and respectfully.
  • Use only verified account and vehicle information.
  • Report incidents, safety concerns or material changes in eligibility.

3. Availability and trips

Riders should only go online when genuinely available. Repeated cancellations, fraudulent activity, unsafe conduct or other serious platform-risk indicators may lead to review, temporary suspension or termination in accordance with applicable procedures.

4. Earnings and commercial terms

Applicable commissions, rider earnings, settlement schedules, incentives and other commercial terms are communicated through the current OkRide rider agreement, platform interface or commercial schedule. They may be changed prospectively in accordance with the applicable agreement and law.

5. Safety

Riders must follow the OkRide Safety Policy. Emergency features are for genuine emergencies. Safety incidents may trigger review and appropriate protective measures.

6. Contact

For rider legal enquiries, contact privacy@oassolutions.com.ng. Operational support should use the current OkRide rider support channel.
