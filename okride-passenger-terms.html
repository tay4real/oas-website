1. Scope

These Passenger Terms apply to your use of OkRide as a passenger. OkRide is a mobility platform operated by OAS Solutions Ltd that facilitates connections between passengers and participating riders.

2. Booking and trips

You must provide accurate pickup and destination information and use the service only for lawful purposes. Trip availability, fare estimates and service areas may change. A booking does not guarantee a specific arrival time.

3. Location and emergency contacts

OkRide may use device location where necessary for booking, navigation, trip tracking and safety features. You are responsible for ensuring emergency-contact information you provide is accurate and that you have an appropriate basis for providing another person's contact details.

4. Safety and conduct

Follow the OkRide Safety Policy and treat riders respectfully. Do not request unsafe or unlawful routes, transport prohibited items, or misuse emergency features. Report safety concerns promptly.

5. Payment and refunds

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. Current fares, applicable fees, refund rules and any promotional terms may be displayed in the OkRide platform or current commercial schedule and may change from time to time.

6. Platform role

OAS operates the technology platform and related support services. The specific legal relationship among OAS, passengers and riders, including transportation responsibilities, will be governed by applicable law and the current OkRide operating model.

7. Contact OkRide

For account or trip support, use the support channel provided by OkRide. For legal enquiries, contact privacy@oassolutions.com.ng.
