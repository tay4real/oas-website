1. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to be informed, access your personal data, request correction, request deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, request portability, withdraw consent where applicable, and seek redress.

2. How to make a request

Send your request to privacy@oassolutions.com.ng

Please include your name, the OAS product or service concerned, the request you are making, the email/phone associated with the account where relevant, and enough information for us to verify the request securely.

3. Verification and response

We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing personal information. We will assess requests against applicable law, legitimate retention requirements, the rights of other people, and technical or security considerations.

4. Complaints and redress

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may use any applicable complaint or regulatory route available under Nigerian data-protection law. We encourage you to contact us first so we can investigate and resolve the issue.
